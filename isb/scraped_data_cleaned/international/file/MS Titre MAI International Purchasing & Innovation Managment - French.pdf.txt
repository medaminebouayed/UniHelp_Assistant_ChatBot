MAI Management des achats internationaux & innovation INNOV ATION & SPEED TO MARKET Le MAI forme des acheteurs leaders de l'innova- tion avec une vision "business driver". Ils déve - loppent des compétences en management rela - tionnel ainsi que des capacités solides en culture et techniques d'achat dans un contexte interna- tional. Ces compétences leur permettent d'as - sumer efficacement les nouvelles missions des futurs responsables achats. VISION GLOBALE DE L’ENTREPRISE Les étudiants sont exposés à des conditions de travail réalistes et utilisent diverses méthodes pédagogiques : études de cas, travaux de groupe, présentations, exercices pratiques, jeux de rôles, rapports de synthèse. Les enseigne - ments transversaux leur permettent d'évoluer dans différents domaines tels que le marketing, la stratégie et le développement de produits. RECONNAISSANCE DES PROFESSIONNELS Les diplômés possèdent des compétences solides pour identifier les sources de croissance, prévoir les évolutions du marché, anticiper les tendances de consommation et comprendre les facteurs d'innovation essentiels pour leur entreprise. Les programmes sont reconnus unanimement par les recruteurs, qui proposent plus de 1 100 offres de stage ou d'emploi chaque année. MSc MAI International Purchasing & Innovation Management INTERNAL & EXTERNAL SKILLS Within purchasing and innovation, value can be created both internally and externally. Students will be exposed to both spheres as part of their learning in the MSc. Skills and competencies developed during the programme include new product design, in-depth market knowledge, new technologies and all aspects of the purchasing process. Innovation and management are central to courses, empowering students to understand how to create, deliver and capture value from innovation. A WORLD OF OPPORTUNITIES The MAI offers an unrivalled professional network, both nationally and internationally. We provide our students with opportunities, networks and experiences for exciting careers in innovation and entrepreneurship. This is the result of close relationships with leading enterprises, which regularly contribute to the programme and enhance students' employability. It also draws on a community of 8,000 graduates, the only alumni community in Europe to include so many purchasing directors. INTERNATIONAL EXPOSURE The international element is central to ensure students learn across cultural boundaries and adopt different perspectives. Taught in English, courses feature top academics from leading global institutions and international experts. The MSc has international opportunities embedded throughout, resulting in a proven record of international employability. The student body is made up of 60% international students from 15 different nationalities – contributing to a truly global student experience. HIGHLIGHTS Time to shake innovation "How can companies work more closely with their strategic suppliers, bring their products to market faster and capture innovation? This MSc, addressing these questions, is recognised by multinationals as being at the leading edge of purchasing and innovation capture for new product development. It is designed for result-driven individuals who feel they are cut out to work in human communication and relationship building in an international environment."
